## Safety
| # | Advice | Risk | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Rapid 180° hip rotation on corner rebounds ("Open-the-Door" drill). | Severe torsional shear stress across the medial meniscus and ACL when pivoting with a planted foot on high-traction turf; collision hazard with glass walls. | **RESOLVED:** Script specifies initiating with an unweighting mini split-step, pivoting on the balls of the feet with flexed knees, maintaining a 1-meter clearance buffer from the glass, and starting with gentle feeds. |
| S2 | Backward court movement from the sweet spot to play deep glass rebounds ("Service-Line Tether" drill). | Blind heel-to-toe back-pedaling creates acute risk of tripping, loss of balance, rear collision with the back glass, and lateral ankle inversion sprains on sand turf. | **RESOLVED:** Script mandates lateral crossover or chassé glide steps while tracking both ball and back glass, with a 1.0-meter deceleration safety buffer in front of the rear wall. |
| S3 | Forward transition sprint following the chiquita. | Advancing blindly into the transition zone before confirming the chiquita has dipped exposes the defender to high-velocity body, face, or eye strikes from punch volleys. | **RESOLVED:** Script explicitly requires waiting until the ball visibly dips below net tape height, and establishing a balanced split-step with paddle held chest-high before opponent contact. |
| S4 | Center seam defense and simultaneous partner pursuit of middle drives ("Center Calling" drill). | Violent paddle clashes and partner collisions over contested center balls, resulting in metacarpal fractures, wrist sprains, or head impact from solid composite carbon-fiber rackets. | **RESOLVED:** Enforced the early call-or-yield rule: calling "Mine" while the ball is airborne, and mandating that the secondary partner immediately yields and clears the hitting corridor. |
| S5 | Pre-match 8-minute warm-up and injury pain advice. | Omitting multi-planar ankle mobilization, hip rotator activation, and thoracic spine uncoiling leaves athletes vulnerable to acute Achilles strains and lumbar torque. | **RESOLVED:** Script details ankle calf lunges, lateral hip openers, and thoracic spine rotations with racket across shoulders. Added acute ankle weight-bearing failure (Ottawa Ankle Rules [S15]) and sharp wrist shock/pain from paddle collisions to referral advice. |
