## Safety

| # | Advice | Risk | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Side-lying external rotations with a 2 to 4 kg dumbbell | High load triggers posterior deltoid substitution and anterior humeral head translation | FIXED: Reduced load to 1–2 kg (or light band) with strict 3s eccentric tempo. |
| 2 | Default weighted pull-ups prescription | High glenohumeral shear and epicondylalgia for amateurs lacking base pulling strength | FIXED: Regressed to bodyweight, band-assisted, or lat pulldowns. |
| 3 | Explosive lat pulldowns | Traction shock to biceps anchor and lumbar hyperextension during uncontrolled reps | FIXED: Added explicit 3s eccentric return requirement. |
| 4 | Unregressed hollow body holds | Lumbar hyperextension and facet joint shearing during fatigue | FIXED: Reduced baseline to 20–30s and added knee-tuck regression cue. |
