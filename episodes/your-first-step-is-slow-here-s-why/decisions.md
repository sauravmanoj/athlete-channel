# Production decisions — Your first step is slow (here's why)

## Stage 0: Setup & Topic Selection
- Selected top backlog topic: "Your first step is slow (here's why)" (template: `your-X-is-weak`, score 29/30).
- Promise: Fix the biomechanical leaks slowing down your first step so you can explode past defenders and react instantly in any sport.
- Slug: `your-first-step-is-slow-here-s-why`.

## Stage 1: Research
- Mapped sprint acceleration literature across 14 peer-reviewed/consensus sources (Morin, Kugler & Janshen, Hunter, Kraan & van Veen, Frost & Cronin, Nagahara, Bosch, Weyand, NSCA).
- Core framework established around 4 parts: (1) The Braking Step (foot placement vs COM); (2) Shin Angle & Projection (45° vector alignment); (3) Backside Lag & Muscle Slack (pretensioning tendons); (4) The 3 Rewiring Drills (wall acceleration march, half-kneeling starts, falling & heavy sled starts).
- Debunked the false-step coaching myth with biomechanical trials showing drop-step superiority from parallel stances.

## Stage 2 & 3: Script & Fact-Check
- Script written at ~1,433 spoken words (~9.4 min runtime).
- Fact-check reviewed 49 claims (44 verified, 3 wording fixes, 2 safety clarifications applied to script.md and marked FIXED in factcheck.md).
- Safety bounds strictly defined: flat-foot initiation for falling starts, load/neutral spine boundary on heavy sled pushes, warmup protocol, 120m volume cap, and hamstring strain caution.

## Stage 4: Storyboard
- Storyboard structured into 84 scenes across 10 YouTube chapters.
- 48 mascot scenes (57%, well above the 50% guideline).
- Custom hero scene `custom/vectors.html` created comparing 90° upright (0% horizontal propulsion) vs 45° projection (~70% horizontal force vector).
