### General safety observations (adequately handled or optional):
- **Medical triage (L97):** Appropriately directs burning elbow pain and sharp shoulder catching to a sports physiotherapist without prescribing self-treatment or diagnostic labels.
- **Racket wrist cord reminder (optional note):** Under FIP Rule 4 [S14], the racket wrist strap is mandatory. Transitioning tennis players frequently forget it because tennis rackets lack wrist cords. While not strictly required for narration flow, mentioning the wrist cord in equipment advice reinforces court safety.
- **Eye protection:** Dahmen (2023) [S11] highlights eye injuries in padel from close-range volleys. The transition plan and script are safe, but sports goggles remain standard best practice.
