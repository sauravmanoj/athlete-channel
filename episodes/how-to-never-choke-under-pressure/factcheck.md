### Title and promise
"How to never choke under pressure" is a hook, and no study shows choking can be eliminated. The title is acceptable because the hook (L12) says nerves won't disappear and L2/L12 now frame the skills as help, not a guarantee. Don't put "never choke again" style claims in the description or thumbnail text.
